You have a dataset — what now?

  1. Train a detector on it. The zip is standard COCO with train/valid/test folders. With Ultralytics YOLO: convert to YOLO format, then yolo segment train model=yolo11s-seg.pt data=data.yaml imgsz=640 — or drop the zip into Roboflow and train there.
  2. Run the false-alarm check. Point the trained model at your raw, clean images. It should find nothing. That number is the one fully-trustworthy metric here.
  3. Be honest about scope. This proves the pipeline and detection of synthetic defects on your parts; real-defect recall needs a genuinely faulty part at some point.
