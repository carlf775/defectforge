DefectForge

Your annotated defect images become the test split, untouched. Your good (defect-free) images are the bases Gemini paints new defects onto for train and valid. Every box is measured from the pixels that actually changed — never guessed from what the model says. Out comes a zip Roboflow imports with the splits intact. Runs entirely in this tab.
